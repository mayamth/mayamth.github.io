Maya Mathur

Hello! I'm Maya. I'm interested in far too many things, but mostly philosophy of mind, interoception, psychosis and functional neurological disorder. Broadly, I study how the feeling of being a self comes from the body, and how it can come apart. Just as often, though, I'm writing poetry and creative nonfiction, going on long walks, and losing at chess.

I love to chat, so please do say hi! :)

These days
  • Based in London, studying Human Neuroscience at UCL
  • Researching environmental risk for psychosis at the MAP-Lab
  • Meeting and learning from patients from all over the world at the National Hospital for Neurology and Neurosurgery
  • Writing poetry and (slowly) my first book
