Etymology adventures

A diary of some of my favourite words and where they come from!

10.09.26

jaundice

From Middle English jaundis (or jaunis), borrowed around 1300 from Old French jaunisse, "yellowness": jaune, yellow, and -isse, -ness.

05.09.26

breath

Spiritus, Latin for breath, gives us spirit, inspire, expire: to breathe in, to breathe out.

20.08.26

refrain

Refrain has two roots: Latin frenum, a bridle, and refraindre, to break, which in Old French also meant to repeat.

12.07.26

mettā

is mitta: friend.
